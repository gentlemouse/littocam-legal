立逗相机支持

需要帮助、发现问题或有改进建议，请发邮件到 1505367906@qq.com。

请写上 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、立逗相机版本和遇到问题前做了什么。截图请先遮住个人信息。

拍了却找不到成片

在 iOS「设置」中的「立逗相机」允许添加照片，再拍一张，到系统「照片」查看。

提词器没有跟着念

联网后打开提词器设置。如果提示语音模型下载失败，点「重试」。需要立即开拍时可以选择固定速度。

Duo 展开后外屏不亮

确认立逗相机在全屏取景。检查设置里的「取景时外屏显示效果」。若已关闭，可用内屏顶栏的星光键点亮外屏。

LittoCam Support

For help, bug reports, or feature requests, email 1505367906@qq.com.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, LittoCam version, and the steps that led to the issue. Hide personal information in screenshots before attaching them.

A capture is missing from Photos

Open LittoCam in iOS Settings, allow it to add photos, then take a new shot and check Photos.

The script is not following your voice

Open Teleprompter settings while connected to the internet. If the speech model could not be downloaded, tap Retry. Choose a fixed speed if you need to start filming immediately.

The iPhone Duo outer display is dark

Open LittoCam full screen. Check “Show on the outer display while framing” in Settings. If you turned it off, tap the sparkles button on the inner display to light the outer display.
