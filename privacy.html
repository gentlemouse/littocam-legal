立逗相机隐私政策

生效日期为 2026 年 11 月 15 日。App Store 发布者为 Rong Yang。

立逗相机在你的设备上处理拍摄内容、导入的素材、台词和语音。App 无需账号，没有广告或追踪，也不会将这些内容发送给开发者。

相机、麦克风和位置

相机用于取景、拍照和录像。麦克风用于录像收音，也用于让提词器跟随你的朗读。允许访问位置并开启位置记录后，App 会把可取得的位置添加到保存的照片和视频。拒绝位置权限仍可拍摄。

相册和语音模型

立逗相机把拍摄的照片和视频存入你的“照片”，保存只需要“仅添加”权限。如果你允许访问照片（完全或只允许所选项目），立逗相机会在 App 内显示你的照片和视频，供你查看和删除。这项权限只用于在设备上显示和删除，立逗相机不会复制、上传或分享它们。你可以随时在 iOS 设置里更改或关闭。导入时只读取你在系统照片选择器中选中的素材。苹果可能按你的 iCloud 照片设置同步或下载相册内容。

开启语音跟随后，iOS 可能联网下载 Apple 的语音模型，识别在设备上进行。模型下载失败时，台词会按固定速度滚动。Apple 系统服务的数据处理受 Apple 隐私政策约束。

保存和删除

设置、台词、灯牌历史、导入的特效、立牌和定格照片保存在设备上。拍摄的照片和视频存入相册后，App 会清理对应的临时文件。可在各功能中删除导入的特效、立牌和定格照片。

要移除 App 的所有本地数据，请在 iOS 中选择「删除 App」。已存入相册的成片可在「照片」中删除，也可在立逗相机的相册里删除（系统会再确认一次）。iCloud 副本和设备备份由 Apple 按你的设置管理。

权限

可在 iOS「设置」中的「立逗相机」调整相机、麦克风、位置和照片权限。若不想在新成片中记录位置，可关闭 App 设置里的位置记录。

联系

有关隐私或删除数据的问题，请联系 1505367906@qq.com。如果你主动联系支持，我们会使用你提供的信息回复并处理该请求。

LittoCam Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026. The App Store publisher is Rong Yang.

LittoCam processes your captures, imported media, scripts, and speech on your device. The app has no account, ads, or tracking and does not send this content to the developer.

Camera, microphone, and location

The camera provides the viewfinder and captures photos and videos. The microphone records sound for videos and lets the teleprompter follow your voice. If you allow location and turn on location tagging, LittoCam adds your available location to saved photos and videos. You can still shoot without location access.

Photos and language models

LittoCam saves the photos and videos you shoot to Photos, which needs only add-only access. If you allow access to your photos (full, or only the items you select), LittoCam shows your photos and videos inside the app so you can review and delete them. This access is used only to display and delete them on your device; LittoCam does not copy, upload or share them. You can change or turn it off anytime in iOS Settings. Imports read only the items you choose in the system photo picker. Apple may sync or download library content according to your iCloud Photos settings.

For voice following, iOS may download an Apple speech model over the internet. Speech recognition runs on your device. If the model cannot be downloaded, the script scrolls at a fixed speed. Apple's system services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Storage and deletion

Settings, scripts, banner history, imported effects, cutouts, and stop-motion frames are stored on the device. Once photos and videos are saved to Photos, LittoCam removes their temporary files. Delete imported effects, cutouts, or frames in their respective tools.

To remove all local app data, choose Delete App in iOS. Captures already saved to Photos can be deleted in Photos, or in LittoCam's own library view (iOS asks to confirm). Apple manages any iCloud copies and device backups according to your settings.

Permissions

You can change camera, microphone, location, and Photos permissions in iOS Settings under LittoCam. To stop adding location to new captures, turn off location tagging in LittoCam Settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or help deleting data, contact 1505367906@qq.com. If you choose to contact support, we use the information you provide to answer and handle your request.
